Our Services

Seven areas of technology expertise, one partner. Explore everything PSP Development offers.

1. Software & Systems Development

From a business website to a full management platform, we design and build the software your organisation runs on: web-based, mobile and fully custom.

  • Website design and development
  • Web-based business management systems
  • Mobile application development (Android and iOS)
  • Custom software development
  • E-commerce platforms and online stores
  • School, hospital and institutional management systems
  • Inventory, HR, payroll, POS and CRM systems
  • Vehicle and fleet management systems
  • Booking, reservation and event management systems
  • Electronic ticketing and payment-integrated platforms

We bring practical artificial intelligence into everyday operations, from chatbots that answer customers around the clock to workflows that run on their own.

  • AI chatbot development for businesses and institutions
  • Intelligent customer support systems
  • AI-powered business solutions
  • Workflow and business process automation
  • Automated reporting and data processing
  • AI-assisted monitoring and threat detection
  • Integration of AI into existing business systems

3. Security & Access Control

We design, install and maintain security systems that work together: cameras, gates, biometric access and identity verification under one integrated setup.

  • CCTV installation and intelligent surveillance
  • AI-powered security and monitoring solutions

  • Attendance and identity verification systems
  • Security system integration and maintenance

  • Domain registration and web hosting
  • Database design and management
  • Cloud-based solutions

  • Network installation and configuration
  • Computer maintenance and technical support
  • IT consultancy and infrastructure setup
  • Cybersecurity solutions and assessments
