Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Academy and our services.

How do I enrol in PSP Academy?

Go to the Academy page, choose your level, pay the registration fee with Paynow, then complete the enrolment form or send your receipt on WhatsApp.

How much is registration?

US$10 for O Level and US$15 for all other levels (Grades 5 to 7, A Level, and adult or career starters).

Do I need experience to join?

No. We start from the basics, and every topic is tied to a hands-on project.

Can I see a class before I pay?

Yes. You can book a private inspection from the Contact page.

How do I confirm my payment?

After paying through Paynow, send your name, level and receipt on WhatsApp (078 019 2367) or through the enrolment form.

Do you build custom software and systems for businesses?

Yes. We cover software, AI, security, IT infrastructure, IoT and digital consultancy. Request a free consultation and we will send a clear proposal.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00.
